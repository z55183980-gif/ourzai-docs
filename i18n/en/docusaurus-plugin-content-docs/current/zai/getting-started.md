# Meet Zai — Getting Started {#getting-started}

Meet Zai is your AI companion on the ZAI platform. Chat about your day, share what's on your mind, and choose how your companion looks and relates to you. You can also listen to spoken replies or talk in a voice call.

**[Chat with Zai](https://zai.gg)**

## Start your first conversation {#first-chat}

1. Visit [zai.gg](https://zai.gg), choose a look, confirm the name, and select a friend, romantic partner, or coach relationship. A romantic relationship requires confirmation that you are at least 18.
2. Select **Start** and sign in or create an account. For an existing account, its saved look, name, and relationship take precedence.
3. A new account starts with a first-meeting prologue. Complete the interaction or skip it to begin chatting.
4. Type a message and send it, for example: “It has been a long day. Can we chat for a bit?”
5. Switch to voice replies to hear Zai, or use **Voice call** to talk in real time. See [Chat and Voice](./chat-and-voice.md) for controls and usage.

If you complete the first meeting and choose a response, Zai may refer back to it when you return on a later day and the follow-up conditions are met. This is not a scheduled push notification. You can change the look, name, voice, and relationship in settings later.

## Account and access {#account-and-access}

Meet Zai uses your ZAI platform account. You may need to sign in again when opening another product, but you do not need a separate account for each product. See [Account, Balance, and Support](../platform/account.md) for registration, password recovery, and help.

The plan page shows what's included in Free and Zai Plus, including voice allowances. These benefits are separate from the USD balance used for the API and ZaiDrama. See [Plans and Usage](./plans-and-usage.md).

## Zai in different products {#which-zai}

- **Zai on zai.gg**: the AI companion described here, with everyday conversation, relationship settings, and memory management.
- **Zai on the official website and in the developer Console**: the platform assistant for product information, documentation, and API guidance.
- **Zai in ZaiDrama**: the creative assistant for projects and canvas tasks.

Each version of Zai has a different role. The instructions in this guide apply to zai.gg.
