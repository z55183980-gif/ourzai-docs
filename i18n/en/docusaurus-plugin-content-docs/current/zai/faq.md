# Meet Zai — Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

## Where do I start? Do I need an API key? {#entry}

Open [zai.gg](https://zai.gg) and sign in with your existing ZAI account. You do not need to create or enter an API key. See [Getting Started](./getting-started.md).

## Why do I need to sign in again if I already have a ZAI account? {#sign-in}

Your account works across ZAI products, but signing in to one doesn't necessarily sign you in to the others. Use your existing email; you don't need to create another account. If you've forgotten your password, use the password recovery option on the sign-in page. See [account help](../platform/account.md#reset-password).

## Why is there no sound, or why does my microphone not work? {#audio}

Check device volume, browser audio and microphone permissions, chat voice mode, and the mute and audio controls in calls. Voice replies and calls have separate allowances. See [Chat and Voice](./chat-and-voice.md#troubleshooting) for troubleshooting.

## Why am I out of voice minutes when I still have money in my platform balance? {#allowance}

Your platform balance and Meet Zai voice allowances are separate. Check the plan page for your remaining voice-reply or call time, reset dates, and add-on packs. See [Plans and Usage](./plans-and-usage.md).

## What if Zai gets a memory wrong or doesn't remember something? {#memory}

Zai saves memories after conversations, so not everything you say appears in long-term memory right away. Check whether **Pause memory** is on, then review the memory bank and correct or delete anything inaccurate. See [Memory and Data](./memory-and-data.md).

## Will changing Zai's look or name erase their memories? {#appearance}

No. Changing Zai's look or name keeps their memories intact. To remove past content, use the memory bank or **Start over**. **Start over** clears chats, memories, notes, and diary entries. Read [what will be deleted](./memory-and-data.md#start-over) before using it.

## Can importing memories restore all my chats? {#import}

No. Import adds memories while retaining existing ones. It does not restore chats or diary entries. See [data management](./memory-and-data.md#export-and-import) for what export and import cover.

## Where can I get help? {#support}

Choose **Live support** from the Meet Zai product menu for help in the app.

Email support@ourzai.com with the product name, registered email, time of the issue, and on-screen error message. Include order details for payment issues. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or API keys. See [Contact and Support](../platform/account.md#support) for more channels.
