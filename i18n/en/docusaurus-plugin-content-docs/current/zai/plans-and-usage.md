# Plans and Usage {#plans-and-usage}

Meet Zai offers Free, Zai Plus membership, and add-on packs. Sign in and visit [Zai Plus](https://zai.gg/subscription) to compare and buy plans. To check your remaining allowances and reset dates, open **Membership** from the product menu.

## Check plans and prices {#plans}

The page compares Free and Zai Plus and shows prices for different membership lengths. Before paying, check how long your membership will last, the total price, and the included allowances. Refer to that page for current prices and plan details.

You pay once for the period you choose, with no automatic renewal. If you're already a member, your purchase extends your current membership. After payment, return to the app and check that your membership status and end date have updated.

## Check remaining usage {#allowances}

Open **Membership** from the product menu to see remaining voice-reply and voice-call time and their reset dates. **Settings → Voice** also shows voice-reply time. The standalone `/subscription` page is for comparing and buying plans; it does not show current-period usage. Reply and call minutes are counted separately and cannot be exchanged.

Appearance generations are counted separately from voice minutes. Custom voice slots are storage capacity for saved voices, not a charge each time a voice is used. Check the page for the amounts available to you.

## When you run out: add-on packs {#extra-packs}

When you run out of voice-reply time, you can keep chatting with text replies. If you run out of call time, check when your allowance resets or look for add-on packs on the plan page. These are listed under **Add-on minutes** in the app; check the pack type to see which feature it covers.

Add-on packs are one-time purchases available only while your Zai Plus membership is active.

Appearance generations included with a plan purchase stack while membership is active. Unused included generations expire with membership, but finished looks remain. Separately purchased packs can add voice-reply minutes, call minutes, appearance generations, or custom voice slots. Included allowances are used first, then the matching pack. Purchased balances do not reset monthly; they are frozen and retained when membership expires, and become usable when you reactivate Plus. Saved custom voices are not deleted when membership expires.

## How this differs from platform balance {#platform-balance}

API calls and media generation in ZaiDrama are paid for from your platform balance in USD. Meet Zai membership, voice time, and add-on packs are managed separately on the plan page.

Adding funds for the API or ZaiDrama does not activate Zai Plus or add voice minutes. See [Account, Balance, and Support](../platform/account.md#credits) for platform top-ups and payment history.

## My purchase isn't showing up {#payment-help}

Make sure you're signed in to the account you used to buy the plan or pack, then refresh the membership page to check your end date and allowances. If payment is still processing, wait for confirmation. If payment succeeded but your purchase still isn't showing up, contact support@ourzai.com with your account email, order details, and when you made the purchase.

See [Refunds](../platform/account.md#refunds) for refund requests and relevant terms.
